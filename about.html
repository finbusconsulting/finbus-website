Who We Are

Business insight with an analytical foundation.

Finbus Global Consulting was established on 13 July 2018 and is registered for business advisory, business development and financial consultancy.

Its broader services include data analytics, financial modelling, tax analytics, and business, marketing and economic outlook for organisations.

Finbus is Nigeria-rooted, Africa-focused and globally relevant in outlook, without implying physical offices outside its confirmed Lagos address.
